Möbius Sphere

Every Möbius transformation is a sphere moving above the plane. A light at the top of the sphere shines its pattern onto the plane. Move the sphere and watch the plane, and the formula, change.

The sphere has radius ½ and starts resting on the plane, so tipping it over gives exactly 1/z. The light is always the sphere's top point, wherever the sphere goes. The white ring is the unit circle; watch it stay a circle (or become a line) no matter what you do.
